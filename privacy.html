Privacy

Last updated 17 October 2026

In short

No analytics, no advertising, no tracking, no accounts. Whatever you enter into a meetup (names, dates, availability, messages) is stored in a Google Firebase database in Belgium, and everyone who has the meetup's link can see it.

Who is responsible

Pio Gino Steiner, Switzerland · info@piogino.ch
Piogino Meetup is a private, non-commercial hobby project.

What is stored for a meetup

  • the meetup's name and the date proposals
  • the names of the participants, as typed in (a first name or nickname is enough)
  • each participant's availability, calendar entries and favourite dates
  • messages written in the meetup

The meetup's link is its only key: anyone who has it can read and change the meetup, so share it only with the people invited. Entries stay until someone deletes them in the meetup; to have a whole meetup deleted, write to us.

Cookies and browser storage

The site sets no cookies and uses no analytics; the cookie settings only control what is kept in your browser's local storage:

  • cookieConsent, cookieSettings, consentTimestamp, consentVersion: your choice in the cookie banner, so it isn't shown again
  • piogino-theme-preference: the colour theme, if you changed it

These values stay on your device. You can change them in the cookie settings at the bottom of the page or delete them by clearing the site data in your browser's settings.

Data abroad

The meetup data is stored in Belgium, which Switzerland recognises as providing adequate data protection. GitHub and Google are based in the USA and may access data from there; both state that they participate in the Swiss-U.S. Data Privacy Framework. 7TV is outside Switzerland and the EU.

Your rights

Under the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection (FADP) you can ask what personal data about you is processed, and have it corrected or deleted. Write to info@piogino.ch, ideally with the meetup's link. You can also contact the Federal Data Protection and Information Commissioner (FDPIC).
